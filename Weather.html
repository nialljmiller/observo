◆ About This Setup

A Raspberry Pi records the local weather conditions and camera images:

  • Temperature & Humidity (DHT22 / AM2302) — Measures ambient air temperature and relative humidity.
  • Camera (OV5647) — Captures the site in daylight and low light using adaptive day, twilight, and night imaging.
  • Barometric Pressure — A pressure sensor will be added; the data pipeline already has separate fields for pressure and its temperature measurement.
DHT22 polling intervalEvery 5 seconds
Weather data blockEvery 30 seconds
Data sent to serverEvery 5 minutes
Forecast modelEnabled after sufficient new station data are collected
