◆ About This Setup

Three sensors connected to a Raspberry Pi measure different aspects of the local environment:

  • Barometric Pressure (BMP085) — Measures air pressure to detect weather changes. Higher pressure indicates clear skies; lower pressure suggests storms or rain. Also estimates altitude relative to sea level.
  • Temperature & Humidity (DHT11) — Measures air temperature and moisture content. Together these describe comfort level and provide context for other weather readings.
  • Light Sensor (BH1750) — Measures ambient brightness in lux, tracking daylight, cloud cover, and changes in surroundings.
Sensor sampling intervalEvery 10 seconds
Data sent to serverEvery 30 seconds

RNN retrainingOnce daily
